# Overview

Alphonse is an agent built for family households with the idea of creating a personal assistant for families. One single agent who can serve the needs of an entire family. It contains skills/personalities which give it appropriate perspectives to solve all family situations. Childcare, healthcare, loneliness, security, domotics, nutrition, coordination, home management, home finances, family health records, vacation planning, etc.

Why do we need such an agent? In a world where AI and AI agents become more ubiquitous, being able to receive help, delegate chores (digital ones), coordinate family members while still keep all family history, trust and context private will be of great value in the mid to long term.

Alphonse is like a digital family home butler who can be trusted.

Alphonse can start as a centralized entity in the household but it can grow to a team of agents who talk and coordinate as family needs grow. Alphonse can also delegate and coordinate with other agents with other specialties to achieve the best results.

Alphonse can expand it's capabilities by learning, dreaming and creating it's own artifacts which reduce token consumption.
