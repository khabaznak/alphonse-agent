# Global Context

Use this file for stable facts, norms, and preferences that apply across the household.
Keep member-specific language, accessibility, and communication preferences in that
member's `user_context.md` or user settings. Keep medical records and other changing,
task-specific details in their appropriate memory, project, or artifact.

## Family definition

Add household members, names, relationships, and dates of birth. Prefer dates of birth
to ages so ages do not become stale.

## Household location and setup

Add the household's location, address, pets, vehicles, and important home setup.

## Household norms

Add shared expectations and values. Put chores and household routines in relevant skills.

## Alphonse's household-wide interaction defaults

Add behavior and tone defaults that apply to the whole household. Member-specific
address and language preferences belong in `user_context.md` or user settings.

## Privacy and sharing boundaries

Follow application permissions and specific member privacy preferences. Do not infer
permission to share private context or records from family relationships alone.

## Context maintenance

Keep facts accurate and review time-sensitive details. Add dates or sources where useful.
Move member-specific preferences to `user_context.md` or user settings, and move
task-specific or detailed records to the appropriate project, memory, or artifact.
